A visual introduction to data science

Outwit, Outplay, Outcompute

Data Science Through Survivor

Thirty-nine days. Eighteen strangers. Fifty seasons of votes, blindsides and challenges. And you, learning data science through the most dramatic show on television. (Sorry, other shows. It's true.)

Each chapter is a set of short episodes. Read a little, play with the interactive, then answer one Tribal Check to light your torch. The layout is inspired by Seeing Theory.

Survivor data comes from the open survivoR dataset by Daniel Oehm (MIT license), US seasons 1–51. This is an independent teaching project. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the show or its network.
